About ROBO INVENTOR

Learn by building.

ROBO INVENTOR is a personal robotics and maker portfolio by Aniket Tiwari. This site documents practical experiments with Arduino, sensors, motors, electronics, and 3D printing—one project at a time.

The goal is to share the process: the idea, the components, the build, the tests, and the improvements.

RoboticsBuild and test real mechanisms and robot systems.
💡
ExperimentationLearn through practical electronics and coding.
